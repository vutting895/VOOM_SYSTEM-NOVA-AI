# EMA12/EMA26 + RSI14 Backtest — 2026-10-24

## Status
Research-only offline backtest completed. No MT5 connection and no orders sent.

## Strategy rules
- Long: EMA12 crosses above EMA26 on a completed M15 candle; close is above the interpolated EMA crossing level; RSI14 is strictly between 50 and 70 and rising versus the prior candle.
- Short: EMA12 crosses below EMA26 on a completed M15 candle; close is below the interpolated EMA crossing level; RSI14 is strictly between 30 and 50 and falling versus the prior candle.
- Entry is simulated at the next bar open with spread and slippage proxy.
- Long exits: 2R target, 2x ATR stop, close below EMA26, opposite EMA cross, RSI >=70 turning down, or max-loss limit.
- Short exits: 2R target, 2x ATR stop, close above EMA26, opposite EMA cross, RSI <=30 turning up, or max-loss limit.
- Because the user did not specify numeric TP/SL amounts, 2R TP, 2x ATR stop, 0.25% risk proxy, 2% of segment-start equity per-trade max-loss exit, and 2 points slippage per fill are explicit exploratory defaults, not final production settings.

## Interpretation
The tested configuration lost money in all three chronological segments; profit factor stayed below 1, including the untouched out-of-sample segment. Do not approve this configuration for live trading. The strategy needs review before any further tuning, and tuning must not reuse the frozen OOS results as a selection target.

## Limitations
- Bar-level OHLC simulation, not tick replay. If stop and target are both touched in a bar, stop is assumed first.
- Spread is a proxy; commissions, swaps, exact bid/ask path, execution latency and market impact are not modeled.
- Risk sizing uses abstract units and is not verified MT5 lot sizing or account-currency conversion.
- These are research metrics, not a prediction of future profitability.
- No live-trading flags, EA settings, account settings or order paths were changed.
